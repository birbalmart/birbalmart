Kyun Banaya Birbal Mart?

Hum chahte the ek aisa store banayein jahan AI aapka personal shopping advisor ho — jaise Mughal emperor Akbar ke paas Birbal tha. Wise, fast, aur hamesha aapke fayde mein sochne wala.
