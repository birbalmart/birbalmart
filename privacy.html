Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 4, 2026 | Effective Date: May 4, 2026

🔒 Birbal Mart aapki privacy ka poora khayal rakhta hai. Hum aapki personal information kabhi bhi bechte ya share nahi karte — sirf service delivery ke liye use karte hain.

1. Information We Collect

Jab aap Birbal Mart use karte hain toh hum yeh information collect karte hain:

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Delivery address aur location
  • Order history aur purchase details
  • Device information aur browser type
  • Payment method (card details store nahi karte)

2. How We Use Your Information

Aapki information sirf in purposes ke liye use hoti hai:

  • Orders process karne aur deliver karne ke liye
  • Customer support provide karne ke liye
  • Account manage karne ke liye
  • Birbal AI ko personalized suggestions dene ke liye
  • Order updates aur SMS/email notifications bhejne ke liye

3. Data Security

Aapka data SSL encryption se protect hota hai. Hum industry-standard security measures use karte hain. Payment information secure payment gateways se process hoti hai — hum card details store nahi karte.

4. Third Party Sharing

Hum aapki personal information kisi third party ko sell nahi karte. Sirf delivery partners ko delivery address share kiya jata hai order deliver karne ke liye. FBR tax compliance ke liye required information share ki ja sakti hai.

5. Cookies

Hum cookies use karte hain aapki shopping experience better banane ke liye — jaise cart items save karna aur login state maintain karna. Aap cookies disable kar sakte hain lekin kuch features kaam nahi karein ge.

6. Your Rights

Aap apna account delete kar sakte hain, apni information update kar sakte hain, aur marketing emails se unsubscribe kar sakte hain. Kisi bhi inquiry ke liye support@birbalmart.com pe contact karein.

7. Contact

Privacy concerns ke liye: support@birbalmart.com ya WhatsApp pe contact karein.
