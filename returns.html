Return Policy

Last Updated: May 4, 2026 | 7-Day Easy Returns

Birbal Mart mein aapki satisfaction hamari priority hai. Agar aap satisfied nahi hain toh hum 7 din mein return accept karte hain.

Return Process — 3 Easy Steps

1
Contact Us Within 7 Days
Delivery ke 7 din ke andar WhatsApp ya email pe contact karein. Order ID aur return reason batayein.
2
Ship the Product Back
Product original packaging mein wapis bhejein. Return shipping instructions hamari team provide karegi.
3
Refund Processed
Product receive hone ke 3-5 working days mein refund process hoga — same payment method pe.

Return Eligibility

✅ Return Eligible
  • Damaged product received
  • Wrong product delivered
  • Product not as described
  • Defective item
  • Missing parts/accessories
❌ Not Eligible
  • Used or worn items
  • After 7 days of delivery
  • Damaged by customer
  • Missing original packaging
  • Perishable/hygiene items

Refund Timeline

COD orders: Cash refund ya store credit 3-5 days mein. JazzCash/EasyPaisa: 2-3 business days. Card payments: 5-7 business days (bank processing time).
