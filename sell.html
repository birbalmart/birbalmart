✦ Seller FAQs

Common Questions

Kitne time mein approval milti hai?▾
Registration submit karne ke 24-48 hours mein hamari team verify karegi. Approval email birbalmartofficial@gmail.com se aayega — baad mein dashboard access milega!
Payment kab milti hai sellers ko?▾
Har week Thursday ko payment process hoti hai — JazzCash, EasyPaisa, ya bank account mein directly. Delivery confirm hone ke baad commission minus karke payment bhejte hain.
Return policy seller ke liye kya hai?▾
Agar product damaged ya wrong deliver hua toh seller responsible hai. Fake ya misleading products pe strict action hoga — account permanently ban hoga. Genuine cases mein platform support karta hai.
Kitne products list kar sakte hain?▾
Basic plan mein 50 products, Standard mein 500, aur Premium mein unlimited products list kar sakte hain. Birbal AI automatically products ko optimize karke customers tak pahunchata hai.
Kya bina shop ke bhi sell kar sakte hain?▾
Haan bilkul! Individual sellers bhi register kar sakte hain — sirf CNIC aur genuine products chahiye. Ghar se bhi sell kar sakte hain. Verified badge milne ke baad zyada orders aate hain!
