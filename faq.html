FAQs

Aapke common sawaalat ke jawab — yahan sab kuch clear hai!

Birbal Mart pe order kaise karein?▾
Bohot simple hai! Product dhundein → Cart mein add karein → Checkout pe delivery details bharein → Payment method chunein (COD ya online) → Order place karein! Birbal AI bhi help kar sakta hai — bas apna budget batao!
Cash on Delivery available hai?▾
Haan bilkul! Local Pakistan stock ke liye Cash on Delivery (COD) available hai. Delivery wala aapke darwaze pe ayega — tab paisa dena hai. Imported items ke liye online payment zaroori hai quality guarantee ke saath.
Delivery kitne din mein hoti hai?▾
Local Pakistan stock: Same Day ya 1-3 working days. Imported items (AliExpress/Amazon): 7-30 days depending on source. Har product page pe delivery time clearly likha hota hai.
Order return ya refund kaise milega?▾
Delivery ke 7 din ke andar return request kar sakte hain. Product original condition mein hona chahiye. Refund 3-5 working days mein process hota hai. Contact us ya WhatsApp pe message karein return process ke liye.
Kya Birbal Mart safe aur genuine hai?▾
Bilkul! Birbal Mart ek FBR registered business hai with NTN number. Hum Pakistan mein legally operate karte hain. Secure SSL connection, buyer protection, aur verified suppliers se deal karte hain. Aapka paise aur data dono safe hain.
Birbal AI kya hai aur kaise use karein?▾
Birbal AI aapka personal shopping assistant hai! Bas usse apna budget aur zaroorat batao — woh best products suggest karega. Screen ke neeche 👑 button dabao aur chat shuru karein. 24/7 available hai!
Payment methods kaunse available hain?▾
Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Visa/Mastercard Debit/Credit card. Imported items ke liye online payment zaroori hai. COD local items ke liye available hai.
Order track kaise karein?▾
Order place hone ke baad confirmation email/SMS milta hai jisme tracking number hota hai. Dashboard mein "Track Order" section mein jao ya order-tracking.html pe apna order ID dalkar track karo.
